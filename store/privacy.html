Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Review with your institution before publishing.

This app is the member portal for the Harvey Sarnat Neonatal Neurocritical Care (NNCC) Fellowship at the University of Calgary. It is an educational and professional-reference tool.

What we collect

When you are invited and sign in, we collect your name and email address to create your account, and we store your course progress, quiz results, certificates, and any content you post in the members' forum. If you request to join, we also collect the country and profession you enter.

How we use it

Solely to operate the fellowship portal: authenticating you, showing your courses and progress, running coaching/webinar registration, and enabling the members' forum. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising, and there is no advertising identifier or ad tracking in the app.

Where it is stored

Account and content data are stored in our backend provider, Supabase, on the program's behalf, protected by access controls and encrypted in transit.
