## Full description
The official member app for the Harvey Sarnat Neonatal Neurocritical Care (NNCC)
Fellowship at the University of Calgary.

Sign in with your member account to:
• Work through the NNCC education modules, lesson by lesson, and track your progress
• Watch course videos and open slides/PDFs on your phone
• Register for live one-on-one coaching and group webinars with faculty
• Ask questions, share cases, and discuss with fellows and faculty in the members' forum
• Earn and download completion certificates

Access is by invitation. This is an educational and professional-reference tool for
clinicians; it is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice.
