Harvey Sarnat Neonatal Neurocritical Care Fellowship

Canada's first UCNS-accredited fellowship in neonatal neuro-critical care, training expert consultants in the diagnosis, management and neurodevelopmental follow-up of critically ill newborns. Choose where you'd like to go.

Curriculum

Fellowship Program

The complete competency-based curriculum: 13 four-week blocks across two Level III/IV NICUs, mapped to the seven CanMEDS roles with defined rotations, milestones and assessment. See exactly how a fellow is trained, evaluated and prepared for independent practice.

Member Portal

The secure home for fellows and faculty. Sign in to reach your enrolled courses, follow your progress, submit and receive graded assignments, earn completion certificates, and take part in the members' discussion forum, everything in one place.

Innovation in Education Lab

Where teaching meets research. Discover the projects, digital tools and studies advancing how neonatal neuro-critical care is taught, from competency mapping and simulation to novel assessment methods that other centres can adopt and adapt.

Learning Modules

Core Competencies in NNCC, a structured library of self-paced modules and a large question bank spanning brain monitoring, cranial ultrasound and MRI, hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy, and neurodevelopmental follow-up. CME-aligned content built for focused, verifiable study.
