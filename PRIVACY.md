# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 July 2026

NNCC-fellowship (the “Project”) — Privacy Policy

1. Introduction
We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal information you may provide while using this repository or related Project resources. This privacy policy describes what information we may collect, how we use it, and your choices.

2. Information We Collect
- Public Repository Data: Information you make public in the GitHub repository (for example, issues, pull requests, comments, commits, and profile data) is publicly accessible and is not covered by special confidentiality.
- Contact Information: If you contact us by email or provide contact details in issues, pull requests, or other contributions, we may retain that information to respond and manage the contribution.
- Usage Data: We do not collect analytics from within the repository itself. If external services (badges, CI providers, or analytics) are linked, they may collect usage data according to their own policies.

3. How We Use Information
We use information for:
- Managing contributions, issues, and pull requests.
- Communicating about changes, security issues, or important updates.
- Complying with legal obligations and protecting the Project.

4. Sharing and Disclosure
- Public content you post to the repository is visible to others and may be cached or mirrored by third parties.
- We do not sell personal information.
- We may disclose information to comply with applicable laws, enforce repository policies, or protect the rights, property, or safety of the Project and its community.

5. Third-Party Services
The repository may link to or integrate third-party services (continuous integration, package registries, or other hosted tools). Those services have their own privacy practices; please review their privacy policies separately.

6. Data Retention
We will retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. Public repository content may remain accessible indefinitely.

7. Security
We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with repository management and communications. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Do not post sensitive personal data (such as financial, health, or government ID numbers) in the repository or issue trackers.

8. Children
This Project is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or higher age where required by local law). If you believe we have collected information from a child, contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

9. Your Choices
- Contact: You may contact us to request correction or removal of personal data you have provided.
- Public Content: If you delete or edit your content on GitHub, cached copies may still remain in third-party caches or backups.

10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will indicate the most recent update.
